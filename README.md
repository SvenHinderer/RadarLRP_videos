# RadarLRP videos

Videos of the localization experiments in
S. Hinderer and B. Yang, "RadarLRP—Part I: Indoor Positioning with Active Radar Sensing
and Passive Reflectors" and "RadarLRP—Part II: Multi-Objective Placement of Passive
Reflectors with Particle Swarm Optimization," submitted to IEEE J-ISPIN.

Each video shows one AMR trajectory [range-Doppler map, detections, and MCL particles over time].
Videos are numbered by trajectory (1–10).

## Demonstrator (Part I, Experiment 2)
- `Demonstrator/Real data/Small_{1,2,4}GHz`: real data, small reflectors, bandwidth 1/2/4 GHz
- `Demonstrator/Real data/Large_{1,2,4}GHz`: real data, large reflectors
- `Demonstrator/Raytracer_reproduction/Lab_{1,2,4}GHz`: raytracing-simulated reproduction with clutter

## Simulation (Part II, Experiment 2)
- `Simulation/Small_room/{f1_opt, f2_opt, int, f1_rand, f2_rand}`: small room, 4 LRPs
- `Simulation/Medium_room/f1_opt`, `Simulation/Large_room/f1_opt`: f1_opt placements for medium room (8 LRPs) and large room (18 LRPs)
